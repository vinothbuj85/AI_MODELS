What is AI Agent? 
  An AI agent is a system that observes its environment, Make Decisions and take actions to achieve goals autonomously. it can be a simple rule-based based bot 
  or complex system powered by machine learning and reasoning. 

Key characteristics of an AI agents :

Autonomous : It operates without constant human supervision.
Reactive and Proactive :  It responds to changes and takes initiative. 
Goal Oriented: Its  designed to achieve specific objects.  
Adaptive : It can  learn from  experience and improve over time.  
Interactive :  It communicates with humans or other systems.
